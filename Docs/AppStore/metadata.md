# Store listing draft

## English

Name: Exergy

Subtitle: Remaining useful work

Promotional text: Claude, Codex, Cursor, and Grok remaining quota — menu bar, iPhone, iPad, Watch. iCloud sync. Tokens stay on device.

Description:

Exergy is a quiet usage tracker for the AI accounts you already pay for. See how much Claude, Codex, Cursor, Grok, Copilot, Gemini or OpenRouter you have left, on the Mac menu bar and on every Apple you carry.

Add more than one login per provider. Focus rings show the first three accounts. Pace marks whether you are burning faster than the window. Your iPhone and Watch mirror the same meters through iCloud.

Tokens live in the Keychain. iCloud carries meters, not secrets. There is no Exergy account and no Exergy server.

Demo meters are available without signing in.

Keywords: usage,quota,claude,codex,cursor,grok,copilot,tokens,icloud,developer
