## 2. Brand — two golds, one family

| Token | Hex | Use on icons |
|---|---|---|
| Exergy chrome gold | `#C4A359` | Exergy glyph only (also `ExergyPalette.goldDark` / `0xC4A359`) |
| Site ΔG tangerine | `#FF9300` | Marketing site, **not** Exergy chrome. Allowed on Shannon/NATURaL only if the existing approved art already uses jewel amber — do not recolor NATURaL bloom to tangerine. |
| Exergy / HUD slate | `#0F172A` | Exergy ground (`ExergyPalette.darkBackground`) |
| Track / surface | `#1E293B` | Exergy spent-portion of a ring |
| NATURaL midnight plum | as in existing bloom | NATURaL only |
| Shannon collapse | deep slate + mint `#45E0A8` (ΔH) or violet `#8B5CF6` (TΔS) | Shannon only — **not** gold gauges |

**Forbidden on every shipping pixel** (site palette v2; exact or near):

- Retired gold `#FBBF24` and pale gold `#FDE68A`
- Retired cyan `#22D3EE`
- Fitness-ring rainbow (yellow + cyan + magenta) — the previous Exergy placeholder

Exergy marketing CSS on thebonhomme.com uses **tangerine**, not chrome gold. That is correct for the site. The **app icon** uses chrome gold so the menu-bar extra matches in-app HUD tokens.

Light/dark: icons are a single opaque asset. Design the Exergy glyph on `#0F172A` so it holds on both light and dark home screens (not pure `#000`). Mac 16px is a **single** thick gold ring + hub, not three rings.

SF Symbols in-app (`gauge.with.needle`, `hourglass`) are chrome, not the App Store glyph. Do not put an SF Symbol bitmap in the App Icon.

Safe zone: iOS important content inside the central ~80%. Watch: entire glyph inside central **68%** so the circular mask has ~8% margin. No pre-drawn circle, no rounded corners, no watch hardware.

## 6. Prompts to paste into Claude Design

### 6.1 Exergy iOS/iPad master (only if replacing the geometric master)

Single square 1024×1024, no mockup, no text, no letters, no border, no pre-rounded corners. Edge-to-edge opaque background exactly hex `#0F172A`. Three concentric remaining-work gauge arcs in chrome gold `#C4A359` with spent-track `#1E293B`, hollow center with a small gold hub. Outer arc ~84% remaining, middle ~61%, inner ~38%, clockwise from 12 o’clock with a small gap at 12. Glyph occupies the central 74%. Recognizable at 40px as “gold rings on navy.” No cyan, no magenta, no yellow `#FBBF24`, no Apple Fitness clone, no sparkle, no drop shadow outside the canvas. One finished square.

### 6.2 Exergy watchOS

Same identity. Only two thicker rings. Entire glyph inside the central 66–68% of the square so a circular crop with 8% margin never clips gold. Opaque `#0F172A` to the edges. No drawn circle.

### 6.3 Exergy Mac 16px

Opaque 16×16 and 32×32: one thick gold ring and a gold center dot on `#0F172A`. No three-ring detail.

### 6.4 NATURaL

Use the **existing** iOS and Watch prompts in `NATURaL/Docs/AppStore/icon-design.md` unchanged. Additionally:

- **Mac 10 sizes:** same bloom, simplified at 16/32, opaque RGB unless HIG for that target requires alpha — if alpha, say so in the PR and add a Mac-only contract.
- **tvOS layered:** Front = bloom + orb; Middle = inner petals; Back = plum field. Large shapes, no hairline petals.
- **visionOS:** bloom, thicker, 1024² opaque.

### 6.5 Shannon Pill (Mac)

1024 master + 10 Mac slots. Metaphor: collapsing probability mass / H↓ as **shape** (wedge or narrowing bars), mint `#45E0A8` on `#08091A` or `#0F172A`. No gold gauges. No emoji. No word “H”.

### 6.6 NATURaL Remote / ClusterFuck (only if shipping)

Watch-first 1024 circular-safe + iOS 1024. Metaphor: a compact control crown or Crooks loop as geometry, slate + violet `#8B5CF6`, not gold rings, not bloom. Store name **NATURaL Remote**.
