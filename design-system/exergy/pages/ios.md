# iOS — phone tabs

Overrides MASTER for `Apps/iOS`.

- **Nav:** TabView with 3 items (Usage / Add / Settings). ≤5 tabs.
- **Layout:** `ViewThatFits` stacks focus meters on 375px; row on wider phones. Safe area + bottom inset.
- **Typography:** Dynamic Type (`ExergyType`), remaining numbers `minimumScaleFactor`.
- **Forms:** visible labels, error under the control, 44pt primary buttons, helper copy for OAuth-not-configured.
- **Meters:** bullet rows for the full account list (chart guidance: 3+ KPIs).
- **Do not:** duplicate the large product title under the nav title; icon-only tabs without labels.
